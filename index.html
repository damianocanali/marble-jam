Build a marble run that plays music

Place the pads, drop the marble, and every pad it hits plays a note. Make up your own songs or solve challenge levels. Made for the whole family.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later

How it works

Three steps from an empty wall to a song of your own.

  1. 1Place the pads

    Tap + Pad and the new pad lands right where the marble will be on the beat. Drag it, tilt it and change its note: longer bars play lower notes. A dotted line shows where the marble will go.

  2. 2Drop the marble

    Press Drop and watch it roll from pad to pad. The camera eases in to follow, every hit flashes and throws sparks, and pads hit right on the beat glow gold.

  3. 3Hear your song

    Every hit plays a note, so the run becomes your tune. A finished song earns up to three stars for keeping the beat, and waits for you in My Songs.

What's inside

Two ways to play, eight holidays through the year, and a lot of marbles.

Create mode, with six instruments

Start a new song, or open Twinkle Twinkle, the starter song that's ready to play. Build with three kinds of pieces: pads, round bumpers and curved ramps. Then choose the instrument your song plays.

Every sound is made by the app itself, with no recordings. With Drums, the pads play kick, snare, hat and tom. Undo remembers your last 40 steps.

78 challenge levels in 13 worlds

Five main worlds are there all year, with 30 levels: First Notes, Fix It, Bullseye, Ramps and Maestro. Eight holiday worlds add 48 more, each open during its holiday. Every built-in song is a level too.

  • MelodyPlace the missing notes so the tune plays.
  • FixMove the loose pads so every note plays on the beat.
  • TargetGet the marble into the cup.

Each level gives up to three stars, and stars open the next level and the next world. Early levels set pieces down close to where they belong, already tilted.

  • Your songs on every device

    Signed in to iCloud, your songs, challenge stars and chosen marble follow you to your other iPhone and iPad.

  • Game Center, if you like

    Sign in from the Account screen for a total-stars leaderboard and an achievement for each world you finish.

  • A little 3D stage

    Pads float under a spotlight, with soft shadows and a shiny marble. Paint the back wall with one of 28 all-year backgrounds, or a holiday one in season.

41 marbles to collect

Every marble is drawn by the game, and the one you choose is the one that rolls. 17 cost nothing: Classic to start, 8 earned with challenge stars and 8 holiday rewards. The other 24 come in six optional packs.

For parents

Plain answers to what grown-ups ask first.

  • No ads, no tracking, no data collected

    Marble Beats doesn't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and the app asks for no permissions, such as camera, microphone or location.

    Read the privacy policy

  • A grown-up question before buying

    Before anything is bought, the game asks a multiplication written in words, like “What is seven times eight?”, answered on a number pad. Ask to Buy works too: the purchase waits for your approval.

  • Free to play, with optional marble packs

    Every level, song, instrument and holiday is included. The only things to buy are six optional marble packs: one-time purchases that change only how the marble looks. No subscriptions.

  • Calmer with Reduce Motion

    With Reduce Motion turned on, the menu demo holds still, the camera doesn't swing and the celebration sticker doesn't spin.
